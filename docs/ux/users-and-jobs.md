# Users and jobs

Who uses this product, where, and for what. From the owner interview
(2026-11-01), in their words; inferences are marked _(assumption)_.

## Users

- **The owner (first user), an active job seeker.** Last edited a resume at a
  desk with a LaTeX editor plus Claude Code. What annoyed them: first think
  what to say (rough), then decide how and where it fits, then what to
  filter it to, then how to format. "I wish I only needed to worry about the
  thinking part - and my rough notes get structured into a well defined
  resume in simple/guided steps."
- **Later: all job seekers** (public once production-ready and secure).
  _(assumption)_ Most are not LaTeX users; they need the same guidance with
  no tooling knowledge.

## Contexts of use

- Phone and laptop. _(assumption)_ Laptop for writing and tailoring, phone for
  checking the score, action items and new jobs.
- Daily, before applying: tailor the resume to one role.
- Occasionally: add new information (a project, a job, a skill) to the base
  resume.
- Waiting is acceptable for accurate results: "I don't mind waiting a bit".
  Sluggish UI, or a long wait with no sign of progress, is not.

## What they see first

"The most important action items - my resume score and how to improve it."
Later: new jobs they can apply to.

## Critical tasks

| Task                                                                                 | How often   | Context         | Effort target                                                           | Source               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------- | --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| See resume score and the next improvements                                           | Every visit | Laptop or phone | Visible on landing, 0 taps; first fix reachable in 1 tap                | interview 2026-11-01 |
| Tailor the resume to a role (paste job, review suggestions, accept/skip, get result) | Daily       | Laptop mostly   | Job in to tailored resume in under 10 taps, no formatting decisions     | interview 2026-11-01 |
| Add rough notes to the base resume (system places and structures them)               | Occasional  | Laptop          | Type notes, confirm placement in 2 to 3 guided steps; no layout choices | interview 2026-11-01 |
| Check job fit                                                                        | Per job     | Either          | Score and reasons within one screen after pasting the job               | project.md           |
| Find new jobs to apply to                                                            | Longer run  | Either          | Later                                                                   | interview 2026-11-01 |

## Non-negotiable for trust

A wrong or useless score or suggestion makes them stop using the app. Show
why a score is what it is, and let the user judge each suggestion.
